The Signature Serves

Three ways to pour it.

Ginger Highball

The clean, bright classic.

  1. Fill a tall glass with ice.
  2. Pour 1 bottle Ginger Fizz (330 ml) slowly down the side.
  3. Garnish with a lime wheel and a coin of fresh ginger.

Pineapple Heat

Sweet, sharp, a little spicy.

  1. Rim a glass with chilli salt, add ice.
  2. Add 60 ml pineapple juice and 15 ml lime juice.
  3. Top with Ginger Fizz (about 150 ml). Stir once. Lime wedge on the rim.

Tea & Fizz Spritz

Lemongrass spice meets sparkle.

  1. Fill a wine glass with ice.
  2. Pour 120 ml Ginger Tea · Thai Spice.
  3. Top with 120 ml Ginger Fizz. Lemongrass stalk as a stirrer, lime wheel on top.

Bar version: add 30 ml of your favourite spirit to any serve.
